01Bring the heat

Marching into the Fire

Thursday, November 5, 2026 · Doors open at 9:00 PM; challenge starts at 9:30 PM · Room 106

Seven salsas, plenty of laughs, and one very brave lineup. Join us for the MIABS Hot Salsa Challenge, where contenders take on hotter bites and questions that bring out their personalities and Federation philosophy. Take the hot seat, taste along, or cheer from the crowd; spectator admission is free. Explore the prizes, contender and taster options, and registration details, and make Thursday night part of your convention story.

02Share your story

The Michigan Mosaic

Convention 2026 time capsule · A MIABS and At Large collaboration

Some convention moments deserve more than a memory. The Michigan Mosaic brings together our voices, photos, stories, and music in an audio and visual time capsule of the 2026 convention. Honor a mentor, celebrate your chapter, share a laugh, or capture what this Federation family means to you. Explore recording options and keepsake packages, and help tell the story we will return to for years.

03Taste & support

Salsa with a purpose

Friday, November 6, 2026 · 11:00 AM–5:00 PM · MIABS table in the exhibit hall

Stop by our MIABS exhibit table to sample a few of the Jose Madrid salsas we’re selling for our fundraiser. Whether you find a new favorite or discover a flavor you never expected to love, we hope your taste test inspires you to support our students! After sampling, visit the Jose Madrid fundraising page linked below to place your order and help send blind students to Washington Seminar 2027, where they will bring their voices to Capitol Hill to advocate for equal opportunity.

Make sure MIABS receives credit: At checkout, select Michigan Association of Blind Students from the dropdown box so we receive 50% of your purchase.
