Every state convention leaves an unmistakable mark on our Federation family. It lives in the electric energy of the banquet hall, the passionate floor debates, the heartfelt hallway reunions, and the laughter shared late into the night. But as the years move forward, memory can fade—unless we preserve it.

The Michigan Mosaic is our living audio and visual time capsule of the 2026 NFB of Michigan State Convention. For our community, audio is our native format—the cadence of a mentor’s advice, a student’s first convention reflection, or a lifelong friend’s cheer carries a depth that ordinary scrapbooks simply cannot match.

Don’t let these irreplaceable memories slip away. Sit back, be part of the story, and let us do all the editing, sound engineering, and visual assembly for you. Ten, twenty, or thirty years from now, press play and remember exactly who we were, what we built, and where our movement is headed next.
